ABOUT

Originally from Ukraine and now based in the UK, DJ RITA is an open-format DJ known for her energetic performances and diverse music selection.

Blending Latin, Reggaeton, Hip-Hop, Dancehall, Afrobeats, Amapiano, Brazilian Funk, Afro House, Balkan, Slavic and Ukrainian sounds, she creates unforgettable experiences that bring people together.
